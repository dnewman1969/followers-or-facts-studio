## Why this chunk matters

1. **#252 is the best single episode of the 2009 investigation.** KF marks April 8, 2009 as the day Soros becomes the name that absorbs everything — Media Matters, Air America, a coming civil war, the demonization of militias. It is falsifiable to the month: Soros's first donation to Media Matters was $1M in **October 2010**, and he said publicly "I have not to date been a funder." Air America had belonged to the Green brothers since 2007.
2. **The same episode contains the confession.** In the Oath Keepers segment he describes using the Tea Party to radicalize people and calls the group "the final solution." Days after the Poplawski murders. Handle the phrase as evidence with the Holocaust echo named, never as a punchline.
3. **#249 opens `A-911-PREDICTION`.** On the July 25, 2001 tape he mentions Bin Laden, and separately mentions the World Trade Center and explosions — never joined. Bill Cooper said more, and earlier, on June 28, 2001. Jones repaid him by trashing him and inventing a story about him cursing on air.
4. **#249 also pays the Y2K pilot.** Cooper confronts Jones on air about causing the Y2K panic. That is contemporary criticism from inside the movement, from the man Jones built his act on — far better than our narration making the same point.
5. **#251 is the authoritarian-wish tape.** A guest argues the country is already under martial law via EO 13603, so Trump should seize the power and bypass posse comitatus. Jones agrees. EO 13603 is a Defense Production Act delegation about industrial preparedness; the Federal Register text is the whole rebuttal. He also claims nobody has ever declared a national emergency except Reagan, with dozens active at the time.
6. **#248 shows the pattern in one move.** A crew calling itself thedarkoverlord steals 9/11 litigation files from insurers and demands bitcoin. Jones calls them good guys in the government. Forbes quoted them the same week: "We're doing this to fuel our Bitcoin wallets." Nathan Wyatt got five years.
7. **#250 → #251 measures the pipeline.** A fake-credentialed Camelot guest's talking points, including the three-senator Fed story from #242, come out of Jones's mouth three days later. Fringe to Alex in days, not years.
