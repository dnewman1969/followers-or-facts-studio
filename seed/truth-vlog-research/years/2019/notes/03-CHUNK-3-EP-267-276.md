## Why this chunk matters

**1. The ladder is datable.** Every other arc we have is "sometime in this period." This one has
calendar dates and a recording for each. That is rare enough to be the backbone of a video.

**2. The doctrine underneath rests on a foreign forgery.** In #268 the justification for doubting a
massacre is Operation Gladio, and the "proof" is Supplement B to US Army Field Manual 30-31 — the
strategy-of-tension document. CIA officials told a House oversight subcommittee in 1980 that
Supplement A is genuine and **Supplement B is a KGB forgery.** It first surfaced in Manila in 1976,
was reprinted as authentic by Agee's *Covert Action Information Bulletin* in 1979, then re-laundered
through the Italian P2 scandal in 1983 so it would look corroborated. Ganser's 2004 book carries it
into respectable print, and Alex reads it on the radio in 2012.

So: **the document used to call American murders fake is itself an actual foreign fake.** That is the
video, and it is nearly self-narrating. `A-GLADIO-DOCTRINE`.

**3. The gun-grab cycle is the real engine.** Before it is a false flag it is a gun grab. The UN
Arms Trade Treaty was the instrument named in 2012 — a treaty whose text covers only international
transfers, whose preamble is explicitly mindful of lawful private ownership, and about which the US
government's own Senate analysis says *"no additional legislation or regulation is required to
comply."* It was signed in 2013, never ratified, and formally abandoned in 2019. Seven years of fear
with no instrument behind it. `A-GUN-GRAB-CYCLE`.

**4. The expert was manufactured, and we can show the assembly line.** #275 books Kelly O'Meara as an
investigative authority on psychiatric drugs. She has no psychology or medical qualification. She
wrote for **CCHR — founded in 1969 by the Church of Scientology**, per CCHR's own about page — and
for **Insight on the News**, owned by Sun Myung Moon's News World Communications. The blame-the-pills
talking point that shows up after every shooting has a church address. `A-PSYCH-DRUG-BLAME`.

And the beautiful part: the official report on this exact shooting found **no evidence the shooter
had taken any drug or medication that would have affected his behaviour.**

**5. Deplatforming rerouted the reach, it did not end it.** `A-ROGAN-LAUNDER`. The sequence inside one
chunk: feud (#261) → four and a half hours unchallenged (#270) → "I made Rogan big" while his
second-in-command quits (#273) → on air impaired (#274).

**6. The lineage is stated out loud.** #276 gives us Dan's thesis: InfoWars is the media descendant of
Militia of Montana, the Oath Keepers of the Michigan Militia, and Oklahoma City is what broke the
first wave — which is precisely why calling OKC a globalist plot is so useful to the people who
inherited it. Pair with `S-DHS-RIGHTWING-2009`, whose worked example of real violence is the 2009
Pittsburgh police murders by a man who believed in gun confiscation and detention camps. **Our own
database already holds that case** (`C-POPLAWSKI-DEFLECTION`). The federal government warned about
this milieu, was attacked for it, and withdrew the report; the murders happened anyway.
