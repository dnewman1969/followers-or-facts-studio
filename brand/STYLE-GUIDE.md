# Followers or Facts — style guide (v2)

## What we are making

Our own documentary presentation. **Knowledge Fight** is a research index only — credited in descriptions and end credits, never as co-hosts.

## Story model: Ken Burns, not lecture

| Burns move | Our version |
|------------|-------------|
| Soldier's letter read aloud | **The claim** — who said it, when, on screen |
| Historian explains | **The receipt** — AP wire, NRC doc, next-day outcome |
| Slow pan on photo | Mood: dates, maps, headlines, owl sparingly |
| Chapter of the war | One **story arc** — how the scam unfolded that night |

**Beat pattern (repeat):**

1. Set mood (one sentence of context)
2. Quote the lie (text on screen)
3. Cut to the document or outcome that answers it
4. Move on — do not re-summarize

## Length

| Type | Target |
|------|--------|
| Default | **5–10 minutes** |
| Network / multi-lie same night | **8–12 minutes** (pilot Y2K) |
| Big arc (documentary debunk, decades) | Up to 20 min if earned |

No filler. No repeating the verdict in every section.

## Combined episodes

When several catalog lies are **the same scam**, merge into one video:

- Example: Y2K plant + Y2K martial law + missiles = **one New Year's Eve broadcast**
- Cuts the list. Raises engagement. Shows the full con.

After pilot ships, review catalog for other merge candidates.

## Short clips (Shorts / TikTok)

Every long video gets a **clip map** in the episode folder:

- 3–5 standalone beats (45–90 sec each)
- Each clip: one lie + one receipt
- Full video description links to timestamps

## Voice

- AI narrator: calm, documentary pace (`en-US-SteffanNeural` until we pick another)
- No outrage. No mockery of believers.
- Aim at **grifters, tactics, documents**

## Credits block (end card)

```
Research trail: Knowledge Fight episode #___
Primary sources: [list]
Followers or Facts — see how they lie to you.
```
