## Thumbnail template (reuse every video)

**Size:** 1280 × 720 px

**Layout:**
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [OWL 40%]    │  LINE 1: TOPIC (big)   │
│   left        │  LINE 2: LIE? / DEBUNKED │
│               │  [FALSE] or [TRUE] badge │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Rules:**
- Max **4 words** on thumbnail (phone readable)
- High contrast — white/yellow on navy
- Same font every time (Canva: Bebas Neue or Anton for headlines)
- Owl in same position every video = brand recognition

**Examples:**
| Episode | Line 1 | Line 2 | Badge |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|
| 001 | SOROS | NAZI LIE? | FALSE |
| 002 | Y2K | NUCLEAR PROOF? | FALSE |

Save Canva template as **Followers or Facts Thumb Master** — duplicate per episode.
