# Followers or Facts — channel voice and messaging

## Name

**Followers or Facts** works well.

It asks a quiet question: are you following influencers, or following facts? It is memorable, searchable, and not tied to one person. The owl + graduation cap reinforces the "facts" side without sounding academic or angry.

## Taglines (pick one primary)

| Option | Line | Why |
|--------|------|-----|
| **A (recommended)** | **See how they lie to you.** | Direct. No ambiguity. Viewer knows we expose lies, not tell them. |
| **B** | **Changing minds back — one debunk at a time.** | Your "changing minds" idea, with "back" and "debunk" so it cannot read as "lying to change minds." |
| **C (banner subtitle)** | **Know the truth before the lie wins.** | Family/rescue tone without panic. |

**Use on banner:** Line A large, Line C smaller in gold.

**Avoid:** "One lie. The documents. You decide." — as you noticed, "one lie at a time" alone can sound like *using* lies.

## About description (copy-paste for YouTube)

```
Followers or Facts — see how they lie to you.

We break down conspiracy claims one at a time using primary sources you can click and read yourself: court records, original journalism, official documents, and timelines.

No face. No outrage theater. Just receipts.

Many families have been pulled apart by misinformation — parents, siblings, and friends who disappear into videos and think tanks that have been selling fear for decades. We are not here to mock anyone who believed a lie. We are here to show the documents so you can help someone you love find their way back to shared reality.

Every episode includes source links in the description and pinned comment.

Not legal or medical advice. If we get something wrong, we correct it in the pinned comment.

Know the truth. Share the links. One debunk at a time.
```

## Tone rules

- **Document-first** — show the paper, then the verdict
- **No mockery of victims or believers** — aim at grifters, think tanks, and repeatable tactics
- **Family-safe language** — "help someone you love" not "your uncle is stupid"
- **Hard topics (Sandy Hook, etc.)** — center court records and harm; never sensationalize victims
- **Politics** — name organizations and funding; avoid partisan cheerleading

## Mission (internal)

Track lies from **conception → mutation → harm**, and the **people and organizations** that profit from them — across 80+ years where the record allows. Short films on that network come later; episode debunks come first.

## Not a "bash Alex Jones" channel

- The product is **the lie** and the **network** (sponsors, guests, think tanks, clip farms).
- Name Alex when he **creates** or **amplifies** that lie.
- Same rule for Posobiec, Dice, Nichols, Beck, etc.
- Older KF "time travel" eps matter because they show **when narratives turn** (e.g. summer 2015) — useful for later short films, not weekly roast content.
