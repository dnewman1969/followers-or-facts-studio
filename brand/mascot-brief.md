# Followers or Facts — Mascot Brief

## Character

- **Species:** Owl (round, approachable — not fierce)
- **Glasses:** Simple round or rectangular frames (reads as "studious")
- **Hat:** Graduation cap (mortarboard) with **tassel** — clearly visible, slightly bouncy when he moves
- **Palette:** Deep navy body, cream belly, gold tassel, warm amber eyes
- **Expression default:** Calm, curious — explaining, not shouting

## Role in videos

- Appears in corner or center during narration
- Points at documents, highlights text on screen
- Reacts minimally (raised brow for absurd claims, nod for evidence)
- Never mocks viewers who believed the lie — invites correction

## Animation notes (OpenToonz / Blender)

- **Mouth:** Simple beak open/close synced to audio (3–4 mouth shapes enough)
- **Eyes:** Blink every 3–5 seconds; look toward highlighted source on screen
- **Tassel:** Subtle sway on head turns
- **Turnaround:** Front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right — one pose sheet is enough for v1

## File naming

- `assets/mascot/fact-checker-front.png` (reference)
- `assets/mascot/fact-checker-turnaround.png`

Generate reference art in Canva or free image tool; trace in OpenToonz for rigging.
